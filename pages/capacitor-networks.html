Capacitor Network Formulas

Series Network

$$ \frac{1}{C_{eq}} = \sum \frac{1}{C_i} $$

$$ Q_{total} = Q_1 = Q_2 = \dots $$

Parallel Network

$$ C_{eq} = \sum C_i $$

$$ V_{total} = V_1 = V_2 = \dots $$

Charge & Energy

$$ Q = CV \quad\quad U = \frac{1}{2}CV^2 $$

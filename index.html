Electrostatics Simulator

Model point charges and continuous distributions to analyze electric field vectors, potential contours, and electrostatic force dynamics.

Conductivity Explorer

Simulate electron drift velocity under Drude scattering, exploring the microstructural impacts of temperature, dimension, and materials on resistance.

Gauss's Law Explorer

Explore electric flux through spherical, cylindrical, and planar Gaussian surfaces, illustrating the direct relationship between flux and enclosed charge.

Capacitor Physics Explorer

Analyze parallel plate, cylindrical, and spherical capacitor geometries, dielectric boundary conditions, and electric field dynamics.

Semiconductor Physics Simulation

Follow bond electrons, thermal excitation, doping, and electron-driven hole motion in one crystal. Apply voltage and watch charge flow.

Measurements and keyboard help

Drag components or wire segments to move them; drag empty space to pan. Snap to grid applies on release; turn it off for free placement, or hold Shift while dragging. Keep placing adds several parts with one tool selection. On the canvas: [ / ] selects components; arrows move by one grid step (Shift for fine movement); R rotates the selected or next part; Ctrl/Cmd+D duplicates; Delete removes; Space runs/pauses or toggles a selected switch. Enter places at the center or uses a selected endpoint. Escape cancels a drag, wire, or placement. Use the Select list for keyboard wiring. Two fingers pan and pinch in every tool.

Voltage is A − B; current is A → B. Negative power means energy is delivered. All grounds connect to the same net; floating circuits use a local voltage reference.
